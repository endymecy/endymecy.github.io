About Me

I am a Principal Researcher and Team Lead at Tencent Youtu Lab. My research interests lie in the area of deep learning and its applications in natural language processing and computer vision, where my work has accumulated over 1400 Google Scholar citations.

I dedicate my effort to three core pillars: Large Language Models (LLM), Agent, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). We aim to bridge the gap between foundation models and real-world applications through robust, open-source tools and benchmarks.
